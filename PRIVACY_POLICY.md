# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

## Overview

SwissGrades is a personal grade-tracking app for students.

The app is designed to work primarily with local data stored on your device. It helps you track subjects, grades, averages, promotion points, and optional exam photo attachments.

## What data the app stores

SwissGrades may store the following information locally on your device:

- your selected option
- your subjects
- your grades and grade descriptions
- your basket selections
- your app settings, such as language and theme
- optional exam photo attachments that you choose to add to grades

## Exam photo attachments

If you choose to attach a photo or scan to a grade:

- the image is selected by you from your gallery or captured by you with the camera
- the image is copied into app-local storage managed by SwissGrades
- the image is linked only to the grade you attached it to

If you remove an attached image or delete the related grade, SwissGrades is intended to delete the corresponding locally stored attachment file as part of that action.

## Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the action you choose, SwissGrades may request access to:

- your camera, to take a photo of an exam
- your photos or media picker, to choose an existing image

SwissGrades only uses these permissions for the action you explicitly trigger.

## Data sharing

SwissGrades does not sell your data.

SwissGrades does not currently provide:

- account creation
- cloud sync
- remote storage of your grades
- advertising
- built-in analytics tracking

Your data is intended to remain on your device unless you explicitly share screenshots, exports, backups, or other files yourself.

## Backups and device transfer

SwissGrades provides app-managed backup/export features that you trigger manually. Those backup files can contain your grades, settings, and optional attachments, so you should store and share them carefully.

SwissGrades excludes its local grade data and attachments from Android cloud backup. On Android versions that support device-to-device transfer rules, SwissGrades allows local device transfer of app data so your grades can move during a device migration. The exact transfer behavior may still depend on your Android version, device manufacturer, and system settings.

## Third-party services

SwissGrades uses standard Android and open-source libraries to run the app. These libraries help with app functionality such as UI rendering and local image display.

At the time of writing, SwissGrades does not intentionally send your academic data to third-party servers as part of normal app use.

## Data retention

Your data remains stored locally until you:

- edit it
- delete it
- clear the app’s data
- uninstall the app

Manual SwissGrades backup files remain wherever you save them until you delete them. Some platform-level transfer behavior may depend on your Android system settings.

## Children and school use

SwissGrades is intended as a personal productivity tool. Users should avoid storing sensitive information that they do not want on their device, especially if the device is shared.

## Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as SwissGrades evolves, especially if features such as cloud sync, export, sharing, or account systems are added in the future.

## Contact

If you have questions about privacy in SwissGrades, please use the repository’s GitHub Issues page or the contact method provided in the public project page.
